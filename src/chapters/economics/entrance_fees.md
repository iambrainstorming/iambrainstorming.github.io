# India's Competitive Exam Economy: A ₹24,700 Crore Burden on Aspirants

Every year, millions of Indians compete for a limited number of university seats and government jobs. The scale of this competition is staggering. Major examinations such as Railway Recruitment, NEET, JEE Main, UPSC Civil Services, and state B.Ed. entrance tests together account for an estimated **2.47 crore applications** annually. Since many candidates apply for multiple exams, the total number of applications is much higher than the number of unique applicants.

If we conservatively assume an **average cost of ₹1,000 per application**, candidates collectively spend around **₹24,700 crore every year**. This includes application fees and other unavoidable expenses associated with applying for competitive examinations.

To put this into perspective, the **Odisha government's education budget for 2026–27 is [₹42,565 crore](https://kalingatv.com/odisha/odisha-budget-2026-27-allocates-%E2%82%B942565-crore-to-education-13-7-of-total-spending/)**. In other words, applicants spend an amount equivalent to **about 58% of Odisha's annual education budget** just to participate in a handful of major examinations.
